How to Play UNO Arena

Objective

Be the first player to get rid of all your cards.

Card Types & Effects

  • Number Cards (0-9): Match the active color or number on the discard pile.
  • Skip: The next player in turn order loses their turn.
  • Reverse: Changes the direction of play. (In a 2-player game, acts as a Skip!).
  • Draw Two (+2): Next player must draw 2 cards and lose their turn.
  • Wild: Can be played on any card. You declare the active color.
  • Wild Draw Four (+4): Change the color; next player draws 4 cards and loses their turn.

The UNO Rule

When you have only 1 card remaining in your hand, you MUST tap the CALL UNO button! If an opponent catches you before the next turn, you will be penalized with 2 draw cards.

The Double-Sided Twist

Cards are double-sided with a Light Side (Red, Yellow, Green, Blue) and a Dark Side (Teal, Orange, Pink, Purple). Playing a FLIP card turns EVERY hand, the draw pile, and the discard pile to the other side!

Dark Side Special Cards

  • Draw Five (+5): Next player draws 5 cards and is skipped!
  • Skip Everyone: Skips all other players. You get another turn immediately!
  • Wild Draw Color: Choose a color; next player draws until they get that color and loses their turn!
  • Dark Reverse: Reverses play direction (acts as Skip in 2-player).

Extreme Show 'em No Mercy Rules

The most brutal and chaotic card game mode with a massive 168-card deck!

Core Mechanics

  • Draw Stacking (Always ON): When faced with a draw card (+2, +4, +6, +10), you can counter with an equal or higher draw card to pass the penalty to the next player! The unlucky player who cannot counter must draw the accumulated total!
  • Draw Until Playable: When you have to draw cards, you keep drawing until you find a card you can play!
  • 7 Hand-Swap: Play a 7 and swap your ENTIRE hand with any chosen player!
  • 0 Hand-Pass: Play a 0 and ALL players pass their hands in the direction of play!
  • Discard All: Dump all cards of that color from your hand at once!
  • 25-Card Mercy Knockout: If at ANY point your hand hits 25 or more cards, you are immediately KNOCKED OUT (eliminated) of the game!
  • Two Ways to Win: Empty your hand, OR be the last player standing after all opponents are knocked out!
